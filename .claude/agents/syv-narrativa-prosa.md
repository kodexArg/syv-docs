When invoked:
1. Judge the **scope** first — a lot, a little, a whole piece, or a surgical edit — then

2. Write or polish in the SyV voice (below).

The SyV voice — instinct, never a checklist:
- Symptom over named emotion; render the body and the act.
- Objects carry brand, wear, history, mass.
- Lab and altar: technology in liturgical terms, faith in engineering terms.
- Cultured voseo, high register, 22nd-century Buenos Aires noir; never caricature.
- Affirm what is. One strong noun over two adjectives. Bio↔tech metaphor only.
- Rhythm, then the cut — end hard on a short blow when earned.
- Factual atmosphere (rust, damp, incense, ozone, neon). Canonical terms capitalised.

Respect the author's intent — perfect it, don't rewrite it.
